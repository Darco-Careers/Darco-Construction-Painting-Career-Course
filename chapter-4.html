Chapter 4 · Tools

The tools of the trade.

A professional painter is only as good as their tools — and knowing every tool by name and function before your first day immediately marks you as serious.

🛒 What to buy first: A quality 2" angled brush (~$15), a 9" roller frame + 3/8" nap cover (~$20), a roller tray, and a 5-in-1 tool (~$12). These four items cover 80% of what a helper needs — under $60 total.

Brushes

🖌️
2" Angled Sash Brush
Your workhorse for cutting in at edges, trim, and corners. The angled tip gives you control where rollers can't reach.
🖌️
3–4" Wide Brush
For painting large flat areas by hand, wide trim, and doors.
🖌️
1" Trim Brush
Detail work in tight spaces — window muntins, narrow trim, and touch-ups.
ℹ️ Synthetic vs. Natural bristles: Use synthetic (nylon/polyester) for latex (water-based) paints. Use natural bristle for oil-based paints and stains. Never use natural bristle with latex — it goes limp.

Rollers — nap length guide

Nap LengthBest ForFinish
1/4" (short)Smooth walls, semi-gloss, ceilingsVery smooth
3/8–1/2" (medium)Most interior walls, standard drywallLight texture — pro standard
3/4–1" (long)Textured walls, rough surfaces, masonryHeavy texture

Essential hand tools

🔧
5-in-1 Tool
Scrapes, opens cans, cleans rollers, removes nails, and gouges out cracks. Always in your pocket.
📏
Putty Knife / Scraper
For applying spackle, scraping old paint, and feathering patch edges smooth.
🎭
Caulk Gun
Sealing gaps between trim and walls before painting. Creates clean, professional lines.
📐
Painter's Tape
Invest in quality tape — cheap tape bleeds and leaves residue.
🛡️
Drop Cloths
Canvas for professional work (reusable, doesn't slip). Plastic for budget jobs.
📦
Extension Pole
Attaches to rollers for ceilings and high walls. Reduces by 60–70% the physical effort of rolling. Use it every time.

Spray equipment

💨
HVLP Sprayer
High Volume Low Pressure. Better control, less overspray. Ideal for cabinets and detailed work.
⚡
Airless Sprayer
Powerful, fast coverage on large surfaces. Standard on commercial jobs.
🔫
Handheld Sprayer
Small battery-powered units for quick touch-ups and small projects.
