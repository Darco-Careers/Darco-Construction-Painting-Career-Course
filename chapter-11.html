Chapter 11 · Getting Hired

Everything you've learned — now put it to work.

If you've worked through this course, you already know more than most people who apply for helper positions. Now let's make sure you get the job.

What employers are actually looking for

✅
OSHA 10 Card
Must Have
The first thing most contractors check. Get it before you apply to anything.
🚗
Reliable Transportation
Must Have
Job sites change. A valid license and reliable vehicle are frequently required.
🎨
Trade Knowledge
Strong Advantage
Being able to explain prep steps, paint types, and tool names immediately sets you apart.
📜
PCA Certificate
Strong Advantage
Employers notice the person who started learning before they got hired.
💪
Physical Readiness
Big Plus
Standing, climbing, lifting all day. Mention your physical fitness — employers appreciate it.
🔥
Genuine Enthusiasm
Most Important
Contractors hire people they want to spend 8 hours a day with. Someone excited about the trade is worth more than any certificate.

Where to find painting jobs

  • Indeed.com ↗ — search "painter helper" or "painting laborer no experience"
  • ZipRecruiter.com ↗ — "painter helper no experience"
  • Craigslist — Skilled Trades — many small painting contractors post here exclusively
  • Google Maps — search "painting contractor [your city]" and call every one directly
  • Paint supply stores — Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, PPG. Contractors come in every morning. Introduce yourself.

Words that work in interviews

💬 Be honest: "I have no field experience yet, but I've completed this course, earned my OSHA 10 card, and I'm serious about building a career in this trade. I'm a hard worker, I show up early, and I'll do whatever prep work the job needs without complaining."

The next level — building your own business

Once you're proficient — once the prep is second nature, the cut lines are clean, and you're trusted on any job — the painting trade offers something most careers don't: a clear, affordable path to full business ownership.

📋
Estimating & Bidding
Chapter 10 gave you the foundation. Go deeper — study production rates, markup strategy, and how to write a professional bid packet.
🏢
Business Setup
Form an LLC (~$50–200), get general liability insurance (~$100–200/month), open a business bank account.
📱
Marketing
Google Business Profile (free), before/after photos on Instagram, yard signs at every job, and Google Ads when ready to scale.
⭐
Reviews & Reputation
Five Google reviews from satisfied clients is worth more than any advertising. Ask every happy customer.
📚
PCA Business Training
The Painting Contractors Association offers dedicated Business Training — Foundations and Accelerator levels.
🎙
The Profitable Painter Podcast
Start listening on your commute. Pricing, hiring, marketing, and running a professional operation.

Once you're hired — remember this

You now have the knowledge and know-how to get in the door. What happens next is entirely up to you. Practice, practice, practice. Every wall you prep, every line you cut, every surface you roll — that's where real skill is built. No course gives you that. Only the job does.

The painters who advance fastest are the ones who show up every single day, pay attention to everything around them, and treat every job as a classroom. That's you now. Go get started.
